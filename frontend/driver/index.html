Approve with BSV Browser

By selecting Approve spending, you authorise one automatic payment to the displayed operator address after your bound session ends, if the final net account is positive. Your total wallet debit, including the network fee, must remain within the displayed spending limit and fee cap. The dynamic pricing rule, fixed conversion rate and expiry apply.

Keep this page open in BSV Browser. After the operator binds your session and it ends, this page requests one payment within your signed limits. The wallet may show a permission prompt. Automatic session settlement

For a driver payment, the wallet prepares and signs without broadcasting. The operator checks the account, limits, expiry and revocation, then submits the exact signed transaction once. A timeout or reload never starts a replacement payment. Chain status is provider-reported, not independent SPV verification.

What comes next

The operator verifies the signed receipt. For advance approval, the operator then confirms your presence and binds your approval to the charging transaction. It cannot be assigned to two sessions.

Your wallet must permit the payment transaction. Keep this page open until settlement completes. Charger admission and budget-based stopping remain separate.

You may revoke approval before payment submission. Closing or clearing this page does not revoke it. Ask the operator to revoke the saved approval; a submitted payment cannot be cancelled this way.
