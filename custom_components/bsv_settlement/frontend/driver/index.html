How charging and export affect your wallet

Charging: your wallet is debited, except when the charging rate is negative. A negative charging rate credits your wallet.

Export (V2G): your wallet is credited, except when the export rate is negative. A negative export rate debits your wallet.

Zero rates add no energy cost. All intervals combine into one final net debit or credit. Current rates are not a fixed quote for your session.
