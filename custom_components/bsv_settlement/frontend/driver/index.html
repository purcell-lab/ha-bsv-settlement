How charging and export affect your wallet

Buy (Import/ EV Charging) rate: your wallet is debited for energy imported to the EV, except when this rate is negative. A negative rate credits your wallet.

Sell (Export/ V2G) rate: your wallet is credited for energy imported from the EV, except when this rate is negative. A negative rate debits your wallet.

Zero rates add no energy cost. All intervals combine into one final net debit or credit. Current rates are not a fixed quote for your session.
